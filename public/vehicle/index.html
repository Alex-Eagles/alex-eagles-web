01 · COMPUTE — FLIGHT CONTROLLER

Pixhawk Cube Orange 2

Every decision starts here. A high-performance STM32 runs the entire autonomy loop in real time — fusing every sensor into one trusted picture of attitude and position and commanding the props thousands of times per second.

Why: redundant IMU stack, deterministic execution and vibration-isolated sensors.

02 · SENSING — POSITIONING

Here3+ GNSS Module

The absolute reference behind every waypoint. Mast-mounted, it delivers global position, velocity and precise timing over the CAN bus; every fix folds into the Cube's Extended Kalman Filter.

Why: DroneCAN-native and raised 9–15 cm above the frame, clear of propulsion EMI.

03 · PAYLOAD — GIMBAL CAMERA

SIYI A8 Mini

Eyes that stay level while the aircraft doesn't. A 4K 8MP Sony sensor rides a 3-axis FOC-stabilised gimbal with 6× digital zoom, feeding imagery down the ground link over HDMI and Ethernet.

Why: the best size-to-capability ratio — stabilised 4K without costing endurance.

04 · COMMS — DATA LINK

Herelink

Three links collapsed into one. A single 2.4 GHz digital pipe carries RC control, MAVLink telemetry and 1080p video together — up to 20 km at roughly 110 ms latency.

Why: pilot, operator and observer all work from one link; the handheld becomes a full ground station.

05 · POWER — DISTRIBUTION

Matek PDB HEX

Every amp passes through here. Heavy copper carries 2–12S / 6–60 V to the ESCs while an onboard sensor reads current to 264 A and streams voltage and current back to the Cube, alongside PWM routing and BLHeli32 telemetry.

Why: the six-output variant on a quad leaves distribution headroom for payload growth — one board instead of a harness.

06 · POWER — REGULATION

MAUCH BEC

Clean power, guarded from the noise of flight. It converts raw LiPo voltage into stable rails for the flight controller, GPS and LiDAR, absorbing ripple, transients and motor/ESC interference before they reach a sensor.

Why: removes the separate receiver battery while isolating noise-sensitive systems from the high-current bus.

07 · POWER — IN-HOUSE PCB

Alex Eagles Power Board

The only board on this aircraft we designed ourselves — drawn, routed, fabricated and flown by the team. It splits the MAUCH feed into dedicated rails: input decoupling, two buck converters for 12 V and 5 V, and an LDO for a low-noise 5 V rail for the most sensitive electronics.

Why: a modular power stage tailored to this airframe — less wiring, cleaner separation, revisable in-house.

08 · PROPULSION — ×4

T-Motor 6007 II System

An integrated propulsion set, matched end to end: MN6007 320 KV motors, Alpha 60 A ESCs and MF22 props — one factory-tuned system per arm across the quad.

Why: motor, ESC and prop engineered as one unit for peak efficiency and reliability.

09 · POWER — MAIN BATTERY

Tattu 28 000 mAh 6S

The endurance behind the mission — a 28 000 mAh 6S LiPo feeding the entire platform through the distribution stage.

Why: high capacity for long-endurance autonomous flights without swapping packs mid-mission.
