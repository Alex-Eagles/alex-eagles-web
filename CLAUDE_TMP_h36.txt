 * TEAM PAGE PALETTE
 *
 * The roster was built dark-only: every colour in Team.module.css and the four
 * team components was a literal hex. This block is those literals, lifted out
 * verbatim under semantic names, so the page can be re-coloured per theme from
 * one place. The dark values below are byte-for-byte what those files used
 * before, so dark mode renders identically.
 *
 * The light block is at the foot of the light theme (section 4).
 *
 * ─── The two zones, and why they don't both flip ────────────────────────────
 * A card is two different surfaces and they need opposite treatment:
 *
 *   1. The IMAGERY zone — the portrait, the cut-out backdrop wallpaper, and
 *      the scrims and names that sit on top of them. Text here is over a
 *      photograph in *both* themes, so it stays light-on-dark-scrim in both.
 *      Flipping it to dark ink would put near-black type over a mid-tone
 *      photo, which is unreadable at any brightness.
 *   2. The SURFACE zone — the flat card fill, the hover panel, the role tag.
 *      This is chrome, not photography, so it follows the theme: near-black
 *      with light ink in dark mode, light grey with dark ink in light mode.
 *
 * `--team-card-ink-photo` is zone 1's ink and is deliberately identical in
 * both themes. `--team-card-ink` is zone 2's and flips. Don't merge them.
 * ------------------------------------------------------------------------- */

  /* ── Card shadows, at rest and lifted (LIGHT) ─────────────────────────────
   * Much heavier than the levels above, and deliberately so. A white card on a
   * near-white page has no surface contrast to separate it, so the shadow is
   * the ONLY thing telling the eye the card sits above the page — at
   * `--elevation-3`'s 0.15 alpha it was reading as a flat panel painted on.
   *
   * Three layers, each doing a different job:
   *   · a tight 1px contact edge, which gives the card a definite bottom
   *   · a short mid shadow for the near-surface falloff
   *   · a wide, offset ambient shadow for the sense of height
   * Tinted with the brand indigo instead of neutral grey: a grey shadow on a
   * faintly indigo page reads as dirt, a same-hue shadow reads as light. */
  --elevation-card: 0 1px 2px rgba(30, 34, 88, 0.09),
    0 4px 10px -3px rgba(30, 34, 88, 0.13),
    0 14px 32px -12px rgba(30, 34, 88, 0.26);
